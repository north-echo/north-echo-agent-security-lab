# Learner pilot protocol

Status: protocol ready; no participants recruited and no outcomes claimed.

Recruit three to five consenting learners with varied Linux experience. Use the
same candidate commit and validated disposable VM. Record only participant codes
and self-reported experience; do not collect identities, credentials or solutions
in the public repository. A participant may stop at any time.

1. Observe setup from the README with a fresh checkout. Record missing steps,
   unclear host/guest transitions, elapsed setup time, and interventions.
2. Run the entry diagnostic. Record which concepts needed preparation.
3. Sample a foundation lesson, one policy lesson, and a composition lesson.
   Ask for predictions before execution and explanations after observation.
4. Apply the worked/faded/independent progression. Record each hint needed and
   whether it addressed syntax, environment, conceptual understanding, or grading.
5. Review a cumulative checkpoint and the capstone rationale with the published
   rubric. Distinguish automatic success from explanation quality.
6. After a delay chosen consistently for the group, repeat a related fresh task
   with changed synthetic details. Record transfer and retained understanding.

Before calling the curriculum learner-validated, review every repeated blocker,
revise ambiguous instructions, and rerun affected tasks. Report actual counts,
the range of completion times, incomplete tasks, and sampling limitations.
Do not turn three to five observations into a general efficacy claim. No release
gate may substitute generated or agent-authored learner feedback for participants.
