# Contributing

Read [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md) for the code/content boundary and historical
MIT permissions, and [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) for development and validation rules.

By intentionally submitting a contribution for inclusion, you offer your
contribution under the applicable project license:

- Software, including embedded code examples: Apache-2.0.
- Teaching prose, documentation and original diagrams: CC BY-SA 4.0.

You retain your copyright; no copyright assignment is required. Submit only
material you have the right to license on these terms. Identify any third-party
material, its source, permission basis and required notices. A link to a source,
AI assistance or possession of a paid course guide is not permission to copy it.

Do not submit employer-internal information, real credentials, private learner
work, completed independent-lab solutions or third-party course content.
Identify material you are discussing but are not offering as a contribution.
Flag licensing uncertainty before asking maintainers to merge the material.

Keep examples bounded to synthetic local defensive labs. For changes to
canonical teaching material, follow the manifest, manual synchronization and
Linux evidence requirements in AGENTS.md. Preserve prior notices; licensing
changes never silently rewrite historical tags or release assets.
