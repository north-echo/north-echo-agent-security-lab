# Safety and reporting

North Echo is intentionally limited to disposable Linux VMs, synthetic local fixtures, and systems the learner owns. Never place personal, employer, cloud, or production credentials in the VM. Never point exercises at external targets.

The lab platform is educational tooling, not a hardened multi-user service or hostile-user anti-cheat boundary. If a cleanup safety check fails, stop and inspect the registered path or PID; do not bypass it with a broad recursive deletion or force unmount.

For a platform bug, record the command, target, distribution, kernel version, and exact error without including any real credential or unrelated host data.
